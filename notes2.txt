- The Hypixel bazaar container are minecraft chest containers
- minescript_plus's click_slot() function is indistinguishable from external functions
like pyautogui because they send identical packets to a server. This means click_slot()
is the ideal choice.
- There are approximately 20 ticks per second (TPS), meaning that each tick last for
0.05 seconds or 50 milliseconds.
- Minescript.execute() executes minecraft commands
